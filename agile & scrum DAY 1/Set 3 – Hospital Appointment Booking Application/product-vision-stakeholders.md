
# Hospital Appointment Booking Application

## 1. Product Vision Statement

For patients who need convenient access to healthcare services, the Hospital Appointment Booking Application is a mobile platform that allows patients to book, reschedule, and cancel doctor appointments, view available slots, and receive appointment reminders.

Unlike traditional appointment booking through phone calls or hospital visits, the application provides a simple, accessible, and reliable booking experience while helping hospitals manage doctor schedules efficiently.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Allow patients to book doctor appointments online.
- Enable patients to reschedule existing appointments.
- Allow patients to cancel appointments.
- Send appointment reminder notifications.
- Help hospitals manage doctor availability and schedules.
- Reduce manual appointment management.

## 3. Stakeholders and Scrum Role Mapping

| Stakeholder | Scrum Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital Management | Customer | Provides business requirements and funding expectations. |
| Patients | Customer | Use the application to book and manage appointments. |
| Doctors | Customer | Provide availability and appointment-related requirements. |
| Hospital Operations Representative | Product Owner | Represents business needs and prioritises the Product Backlog. |
| Scrum Facilitator | Scrum Master | Facilitates Scrum events and removes impediments. |
| Mobile App Developers | Developer | Develop mobile application features. |
| Backend Developers | Developer | Develop APIs, business logic, and database integration. |
| QA Engineers | Developer | Test functionality and verify quality. |

## 4. Role Explanation

### Product Owner
Represents the hospital's business needs, manages the Product Backlog, and prioritises features based on business value.

### Scrum Master
Supports the Scrum Team, facilitates Scrum events, and helps remove development blockers.

### Developers
Design, develop, integrate, and test the application.

### Customers
Patients, doctors, and hospital management who provide requirements and feedback.
