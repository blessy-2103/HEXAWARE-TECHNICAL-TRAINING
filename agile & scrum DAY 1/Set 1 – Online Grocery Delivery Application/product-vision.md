
# Product Vision – Online Grocery Delivery Application

## Product Vision Statement

For customers who want convenient grocery shopping, the Online Grocery Delivery Application is a digital shopping platform that allows users to browse groceries, place orders, make payments, and receive deliveries at home.

Unlike traditional supermarket shopping, our application saves time, supports convenient delivery scheduling, and provides a simple and reliable shopping experience.

## Business Goal

Enable a regional supermarket chain to offer online grocery ordering and home delivery, improve customer convenience, and make grocery shopping accessible from anywhere.

## Target Users

- Customers who want to purchase groceries online.
- Customers who prefer home delivery.
- Customers who want to schedule deliveries and track orders.

## Scrum Roles

| Scrum Role | Responsibility | Anti-pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Product Owner | Maximises product value and manages/prioritises the Product Backlog. | Accepts every stakeholder request without prioritisation. |
| Scrum Master | Helps the team understand Scrum and removes impediments. | Acts as a manager who assigns tasks to developers. |
| Developers | Build a usable Increment and maintain quality. | Work in silos without collaboration. |
