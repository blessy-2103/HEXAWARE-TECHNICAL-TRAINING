
# INVEST Analysis – Online Grocery Delivery Application

## INVEST Principles

- I – Independent: Stories should be independently implementable.
- N – Negotiable: Details can be discussed and refined.
- V – Valuable: Stories should provide value to users.
- E – Estimable: Developers should be able to estimate effort.
- S – Small: Stories should be small enough to complete in a Sprint.
- T – Testable: Stories should have clear verification conditions.

## Weak Story 1

### Original Story
As a customer, I want a grocery application, so that I can buy groceries.

### Problems
- Too broad.
- Not estimable.
- Not small.
- Not testable.

### Rewritten Story
As a customer, I want to search groceries by product name, so that I can quickly find the item I need.

## Weak Story 2

### Original Story
As a customer, I want to manage everything in my cart, so that shopping becomes easy.

### Problems
- "Everything" is ambiguous.
- Contains multiple functionalities.
- Difficult to estimate.
- Difficult to test.

### Rewritten Story
As a customer, I want to increase or decrease product quantities in my cart, so that I can adjust the number of groceries I purchase.

## Conclusion

The rewritten stories have a clearer scope, measurable outcomes, and better alignment with INVEST principles.
