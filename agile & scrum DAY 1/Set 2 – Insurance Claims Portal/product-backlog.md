
# Product Backlog – Insurance Claims Self-Service Portal

## Epic 1: Claim Registration

### US01 – Start a Claim
As an insurance customer, I want to start a new claim, so that I can report an insurance incident.

### US02 – Enter Claim Details
As an insurance customer, I want to enter incident details, so that the insurer can understand my claim.

### US03 – Validate Claim Information
As an insurance customer, I want the portal to validate my claim information, so that I can correct errors before submission.

### US04 – Submit Claim
As an insurance customer, I want to submit my completed claim, so that the insurer can begin processing it.

## Epic 2: Document Upload

### US05 – Upload Supporting Documents
As an insurance customer, I want to upload supporting documents, so that I can provide evidence for my claim.

### US06 – View Uploaded Documents
As an insurance customer, I want to view my uploaded documents, so that I can confirm the correct files were submitted.

### US07 – Replace an Uploaded Document
As an insurance customer, I want to replace an uploaded document, so that I can correct an incorrect or outdated file.

## Epic 3: Claim Tracking

### US08 – View Claim Status
As an insurance customer, I want to view my claim status, so that I can understand its current processing stage.

### US09 – View Claim Timeline
As an insurance customer, I want to view the claim processing timeline, so that I can understand previous claim activities.

### US10 – View Pending Actions
As an insurance customer, I want to view pending actions on my claim, so that I can complete any required steps.

## Epic 4: Settlement Notifications

### US11 – Receive Claim Status Notifications
As an insurance customer, I want to receive claim status notifications, so that I stay informed about claim progress.

### US12 – Receive Settlement Notification
As an insurance customer, I want to receive a settlement notification, so that I know when my claim decision is available.

### US13 – View Settlement Details
As an insurance customer, I want to view settlement details, so that I can understand the settlement decision and amount.

### US14 – Acknowledge Settlement Decision
As an insurance customer, I want to acknowledge the settlement decision, so that the insurer can record that I have received the decision.
