
# Insurance Claims Self-Service Portal

## Product Vision

For insurance customers who need to submit and monitor claims, the Insurance Claims Self-Service Portal is a digital platform that enables customers to register claims, upload supporting documents, track claim progress, and receive settlement notifications.

Unlike traditional claim processing that requires repeated phone calls and manual paperwork, the portal provides transparency, convenience, faster communication, and improved customer experience.

## Waterfall vs Agile

| Waterfall | Agile |
|---|---|
| Sequential development phases | Iterative and incremental development |
| Requirements fixed at the beginning | Requirements evolve through feedback |
| Testing mainly follows development | Testing occurs continuously |
| Changes can be expensive | Changes are accommodated through backlog refinement |
| Customer feedback often comes late | Customer feedback is obtained regularly |
| Delivery generally occurs at the end | Working increments are delivered frequently |

## Why Agile Suits This Programme

Agile is suitable for the insurance claims portal because customer expectations, regulatory requirements, and claim-processing policies may change during development. Agile allows the team to deliver features incrementally, such as claim registration first, followed by document upload, claim tracking, and settlement notifications.

Frequent Sprint Reviews help insurance stakeholders validate functionality and provide feedback early. Continuous testing improves quality and helps identify validation or security issues before release. Agile also supports prioritisation of essential claim-processing capabilities and allows the organisation to respond to changing customer needs.

Therefore, Agile provides flexibility, early value delivery, transparency, and continuous improvement for this programme.
